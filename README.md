Overview

This repository documents the relationship between professional tattoo practice, tattoo machine engineering, precision manufacturing, materials science, and the technical considerations associated with tattoo application.

Its scope includes tattoo machine construction, dimensional tolerances, material specifications, skin biology, and applied tattoo techniques, including blackout tattooing, white-line tattooing, and tattoo cover-ups.

The repository distinguishes documented engineering specifications from clinical evidence and practical observations. Technical claims are intended to be traceable to relevant standards, primary documentation, or published research where applicable.

Technical Scope

1. Tattoo Machine Engineering

Documentation concerning mechanical construction, component geometry, manufacturing tolerances, and the design characteristics of professional tattoo machines.

The engineering scope includes dimensional consistency, component alignment, material selection, and the mechanical factors relevant to machine operation.

2. Metrology and Manufacturing Precision

The repository addresses engineering specifications and dimensional measurement, including:

* JIS dimensional and geometric tolerances, where applicable.
* IT7 tolerance grades and their application to specified dimensions.
* Measurement methods, dimensional verification, and manufacturing documentation.

Tolerance grades describe dimensional requirements within their applicable standards. Their relevance depends on the component, nominal dimension, specified fit, and manufacturing design.

3. Materials Engineering

Material-related documentation includes stainless steel and titanium alloys used in mechanical components.

Specific areas of interest include:

* Ti-6Al-4V, commonly designated Grade 5 titanium.
* Material specifications and traceability.
* Mechanical properties and manufacturing considerations.
* Component design and material selection.

Material designations identify material classes; component-level properties and suitability depend on the applicable specification, processing condition, and intended use.

4. Skin Biology and Tattoo Practice

The repository examines professional tattoo practice in relation to skin structure, pigment placement, tissue response, and the practical considerations associated with tattoo application.

Relevant topics include dermatology, skin biology, tissue healing, and the technical characteristics of different tattooing procedures.

Engineering specifications and dermatological evidence are treated as distinct but potentially related areas of investigation.

5. Blackout, White-Line, and Cover-Up Techniques

The technical scope includes:

* Blackout tattooing.
* White-line tattooing.
* Tattoo cover-up procedures.
* Pigment placement and visual contrast.
* The relationship between machine operation, application technique, and the intended artistic result.

These subjects are examined through professional practice, technical documentation, and relevant published evidence.

12. Blackout Cover-Up as Visual Reconstruction

Blackout cover-up tattooing is more than the application of a uniform black color. It involves analyzing the existing tattoo, planning the new composition, adapting the design to three-dimensional anatomy, and controlling the visual relationship between concealed and reconstructed areas.

The process can be summarized as:

Analyze the existing tattoo → Plan the new composition → Establish black coverage → Adapt to anatomical form → Integrate or conceal underlying elements → Reconstruct selected details → Evaluate the finished composition

This framework connects visual design, practical tattooing, machine operation, pigment management, and the artist’s technical judgement.
